Composition • Recording • Mixing • Mastering • Electronics

Title: Zero Division
Artist: JD Viljoen
Genre: Soundtrack
Year: 2017

Original soundtrack that I composed for an action video game or epic film. With broad influences from Doom (Game) to old school sci-fi soundtracks. The track is mostly driven by organic and distorted instruments to create the immense energy, but combined with electronic textures to create the sci-fi/spacey feel. The tone is very dark and menacing but with influences of surrealism to spark that scariness/eeriness of complete uncertainty.

The whole production was composed, recorded, produced, mixed and mastered by myself. I used Pro Tools for everything and mostly ITB production.

This was a really fun project and I experimented a lot with incorporating the futuristic electronic/digital aspect into the explosive, epic, organic backbone of the soundtrack.

NOTE: For best results, this soundtrack is meant to be enjoyed on a full range stereo speaker system.
