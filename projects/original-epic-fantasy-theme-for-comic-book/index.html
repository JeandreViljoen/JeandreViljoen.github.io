A musical commission project. The requirement was mixing elements and inspirations from multiple different reference tracks to produce musical character themes for comic book heroes and villains. The score features a hybrid mix between electronic and organic sounds / instruments.

An extremely rewarding composition and a very happy client.

The first half of the track features the “good guys” theme with feminine, heroic and pleasant timbres.
While the second half of the track represents the ruthless, overwhelming evil from the “bad guys”
The client wanted a whimsical, action-packed and magical fantasy-based timbre for the themes.

This was the main reference but the client wanted a “less eastern” tonality while still keeping the electronic instrumentation.

Here is another strong example of the organic choirs, war-drum style percussion and high energy / pacing that the client wanted to capture.
