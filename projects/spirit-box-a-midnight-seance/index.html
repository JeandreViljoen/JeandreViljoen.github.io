Quirky weird narrative puzzle game

Play build on itch.io

Step into the eccentric world of a psychic who may or may not be in over their head.

In Spirit Box: A Midnight Seance, you’ll balance quirky clients, questionable seances, and the pressure of earning enough coins to safely guide spirits to the afterlife. But beware, push your luck too far, and you might expose yourself as a fraud or, worse, let the spirits possess you!
Featuring dark humor, bizarre characters, and a tongue-in-cheek take on the supernatural, this narrative-driven game will have you laughing, cringing, and questioning your career choices.
Can you survive the night and pay your ghostly debts, or will the afterlife come for you?

Submission for the 2024 Free Lives 48h Game Jam where we won best Art.

  • Development and Audio by me.
  • Art and writing by Nix Bee.
